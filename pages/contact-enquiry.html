Quick FAQs

How do I enroll in a course?
Browse our courses page, click "Enroll Now", log in or create an account, choose full or EMI payment, and pay via Razorpay to get instant access.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, Net Banking, Wallets (via Razorpay), Bank Transfer, and Cash at our office. EMI is available on select courses.
Can I get a refund?
Refunds are processed within 7 days of request if within the first 3 days of enrollment. Please contact us for specific cases.
How are live classes conducted?
All live classes are conducted via Google Meet. Links are sent by email before each session. Recordings are uploaded to your dashboard after class.
How do I become a mentor?
Visit our Mentor Signup page, complete your profile, upload credentials, and await admin approval.
