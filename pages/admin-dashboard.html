    • Toggle Is Paid to ON → the internship card shows a "Pay & Apply" button on the public page.
    • Set Amount → that ₹ value is loaded by Razorpay when a student clicks pay.
    • Leave amount blank or 0 for free internships (the toggle must also be OFF).
    • Changes are saved per-row when you click Save.
